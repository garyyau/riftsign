# Riftsign test players

Sixteen fictional people for evaluating the quiz. An agent role-plays each one, takes the quiz as that person, then says whether the recommended Legends fit. A separate expert picks the Legends that should suit each player without seeing the quiz results. Each player has their own file, so a role-play agent reads only its own character.

1. Dani Okafor: fast, consistent aggro who wants the game over by turn five. [players/dani.md](players/dani.md)
2. Wen Liu: veteran control player who answers everything and hates luck. [players/wen.md](players/wen.md)
3. Priya Raman: combo tinkerer who builds engines out of odd cards. [players/priya.md](players/priya.md)
4. Marcus Bell: calls himself a mastermind, plays big units every turn. [players/marcus.md](players/marcus.md)
5. Sofía Herrera: quick, pressuring trickster who loves bluffs and gambles. [players/sofia.md](players/sofia.md)
6. Kai Tanaka: loves how Fury looks, plays slow and careful. [players/kai.md](players/kai.md)
7. Tomasz Nowak: casual newcomer with only faint preferences. [players/tomasz.md](players/tomasz.md)
8. Hannah Moreau: competitive, flexible, plays whatever wins. [players/hannah.md](players/hannah.md)
9. Rafael Costa: loves Fury damage and Calm stuns together. [players/rafael.md](players/rafael.md)
10. Grace Mwangi: no favourite Domain, but refuses Order. [players/grace.md](players/grace.md)
11. Jonah Reyes: aggro player who lives for gambles and all-in swings. [players/jonah.md](players/jonah.md)
12. Ingrid Solberg: ramps into huge units while drawing cards. [players/ingrid.md](players/ingrid.md)
13. Theo Park: ten days in, answers from Clash Royale and Fortnite. [players/theo.md](players/theo.md)
14. Mateo Silva: experienced, enjoys almost everything, mild preferences. [players/mateo.md](players/mateo.md)
15. Nadia Petrova: patient go-wide player who sacrifices tokens for value. [players/nadia.md](players/nadia.md)
16. Elliot Byrne: slow, reactive control player who plays for Hidden cards and bluffs. [players/elliot.md](players/elliot.md)

These players were written without reference to the quiz's questions or scoring. Play them as people. Where a self-description disagrees with how they actually play, answer the way the person would answer, which is not always the accurate way.
