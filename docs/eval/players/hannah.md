## 8. Hannah Moreau

She plays whatever wins, and she'll tell you why the thing you like is worse.

**Background.** Ten months into Riftbound. She's placed in the top eight at two regional events and tracks the meta closely. She played LoL competitively in college, a Diamond ADC who picked whatever was strong that patch. She also plays Magic competitively and some chess online. She plays to win, and she enjoys the preparation: sideboarding, matchup notes, studying lists.

**How she describes herself.** "I don't have a favourite Domain. I play the best deck. I like interactive games where the better player wins. I'll play anything as long as it's not purely luck."

**How she actually plays.** Mostly accurate, with one blind spot. She really has no Domain loyalty and has played four or five different pairs across sets. She likes games with lots of meaningful decisions, trading on Battlefields and deciding when to push versus hold. Her strongest results have come from flexible midrange-to-tempo decks that can play either role depending on the matchup. She dislikes both extremes. All-in aggro feels like it plays itself, and hard control feels like it takes all night. She hates high-variance cards and big gambles because they take the result out of her hands. She reads the board well and switches between leading and reacting mid-game. The blind spot is that she claims not to care about the champion, but she has quietly stuck with decks whose Legends look and feel "competitive". A cutesy Legend puts her off even if it's strong.

**What would feel wrong.** A recommendation based on theme or fun with no competitive credibility. Anything narrow that only wins one way. A deck that's mostly luck, however exciting. She'd also push back on being told she's "a control player" or "an aggro player", since she sees herself as flexible.
