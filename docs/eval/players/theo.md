## 13. Theo Park

He's played five games of Riftbound and about ten thousand of Clash Royale.

**Background.** Ten days into Riftbound. His roommate taught him, and every game so far has been with the roommate's spare starter deck. He has never played LoL. He watched Arcane once and liked Vi and Ekko. He plays Clash Royale seriously on his phone, played the Pokémon TCG as a kid, and plays Fortnite and Minecraft with friends. He plays for fun, and he wants to get good.

**How he describes himself.** "I'm new, so I don't really know yet. In Clash I'm a push guy. I send a big tank and stack stuff behind it. I like being the one attacking. I hate turtling."

**How he actually plays.** His real Riftbound experience is thin. He still asks what Deflect does and sometimes forgets his Legend has an ability. When asked about a Riftbound situation he hasn't been in, he answers from Clash Royale or Fortnite, not from the game in front of him. From those games he likes pressure, he likes a tank with support units behind it, and he counts his resources carefully. He doesn't like gambling in any of them. In his five Riftbound games he attacked a lot, sometimes badly, and got flustered when his roommate had reactions ready. He knows the Domains mostly as colours. He likes the orange one because its big units remind him of Clash tanks. Right now he can handle a few decisions a turn, though he thinks he'd enjoy more once he learns. When he's behind he pushes harder, the way he would in Clash. He prefers units to spells, because spells mean reading.

**What would feel wrong.** A deck with lots of text to read or several reactions to hold. A slow deck where he spends turns waiting. He's so new that he'd accept most things as long as he gets to attack and can understand his cards.
