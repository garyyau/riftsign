## 6. Kai Tanaka

They love how Fury looks and hate how Fury plays.

**Background.** Two months into Riftbound. They play LoL casually in ARAM and some mid, and they're a huge Arcane fan. Jinx is their favourite character in anything, and they own the plush. They also love Into the Breach, Slay the Spire on high Ascension and slow-paced XCOM runs. They play for fun and for the feeling of having figured a game out.

**How they describe themselves.** "I'm a Fury player. I love the chaos, the explosions, the red cards. Give me the reckless stuff. I want my deck to feel like Jinx."

**How they actually play.** Their taste in flavour conflicts with how they play. They bought into Fury because of the art and characters, then kept losing because they wouldn't attack until they felt safe. In practice they hold cards, wait for the opponent to commit, and only attack once they've counted every possible response. Their best games were the ones where they borrowed a friend's Calm deck and won a long game through Reactions and Tank units. They didn't love how it looked but said it "just made sense". They like long games, lots of decisions and careful sequencing. They dislike big gambles and will pass on a risky attack even when it would probably win. When behind, they turtle and look for a way to stabilise. Spells versus units doesn't matter to them much, as long as the cards give them options.

**What would feel wrong.** Two ways. A fast, attack-every-turn Fury deck would look right and feel terrible to play, and they'd quit it within a few games. A patient deck with no connection to anything they love would feel like homework, even if it wins. The ideal pick for them keeps some Fury flavour, or at least an appealing champion, while playing slowly.
