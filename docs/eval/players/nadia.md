## 15. Nadia Petrova

She fills the board with little things and makes sure each one dies usefully.

**Background.** Six months into Riftbound. She plays at a store on weekends and against her flatmate most evenings. In LoL she plays Yorick, Heimerdinger and Zyra, champions that fill the map with summons and turrets. She plays Factorio and Frostpunk, and likes any game where a system grows a bit every turn. She plays to win, but most of the satisfaction comes from watching her board get wider.

**How she describes herself.** "I like going wide. Lots of small units, tokens, and when one dies I get something for it. I'm not in a hurry. I'd rather build a board nobody can get through than race you."

**How she actually plays.** Accurate. Order's tokens, Legion, Deathknell and sacrifice cards are her favourites. She makes a token every turn she can and holds Battlefields behind a wall of small units. She trades them happily, because each death pays her something. She's patient. She attacks only once her board is clearly bigger, and she's fine with games that run long. She enjoys the steady decisions of which unit to sacrifice and when, but she doesn't want to hold a hand full of reactions. She dislikes gambles and wants a grind she can count on. When she's behind she blocks with tokens, cashes in Deathknells, and digs in. She plays far more units than spells, and most of her spells kill things. A single big unit feels risky to her, because one removal spell and it's gone. Her value comes in a steady trickle, not in one combo turn.

**What would feel wrong.** A fast deck that races with tokens would have the right tools at the wrong speed. A deck built on one or two huge units would feel fragile. A combo deck with one big payoff turn would miss what she enjoys, which is the board growing a little every turn.
