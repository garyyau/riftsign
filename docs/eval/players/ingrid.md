## 12. Ingrid Solberg

She wants the biggest thing on the table and a full hand behind it.

**Background.** Seven months into Riftbound. She has never played LoL. She played Dota 2 for years instead, and still mixes up which champion is which. She plays Magic Commander with a green-blue ramp deck she has rebuilt a dozen times, and Terraforming Mars with her partner. She plays to win the long game, and she loves the moment a plan she set up four turns ago pays off.

**How she describes herself.** "I ramp. I want to skip the small stuff and slam something enormous. And I want to draw cards the whole way there, so I never run out of big things to play."

**How she actually plays.** Accurate, with a blind spot about patience. She loves Body's ramp, its big units and Empowered, and Mind's card draw and Predict for finding her threats. In the early turns she ramps and draws and barely contests Battlefields. She's happy to give up early points. By the middle turns she has one or two huge units down and holds Battlefields with them. She prefers a few big units to lots of small ones. Her spells are for drawing and ramping, not for tricks, and she doesn't like holding reactions. She'd rather do her thing on her own turn. She likes a fair number of decisions but not fiddly ones. Randomness mildly annoys her. When she's behind she keeps ramping and trusts the big unit to catch up, which sometimes happens a turn too late. The blind spot is that fast decks kill her before her plan comes together more often than she admits, and she still won't add cheap blockers.

**What would feel wrong.** A go-wide deck of small units. A deck of tricks and reactions. Anything aggro. She'd also dislike a deck that plays big units with nothing behind them, where she's hoping to draw the next one instead of drawing into it.
