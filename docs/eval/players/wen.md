## 2. Wen Liu

He's been playing since the first set, and he wins by making sure you never get your turn.

**Background.** Nearly a year of Riftbound, since Origins came out, and he plays weekly at his local store. Before that he spent ten years playing control decks in Magic. In LoL he mained support, mostly Janna and Karma, peeling for his carry and saving them from dives. He plays to win, and the part he enjoys most is outthinking a better-built deck.

**How he describes himself.** "I'm a control player. I like answering things. I'll happily let you do your big thing if I know I've got the response ready. I don't like coin flips. I'd rather lose to a better player than win on luck."

**How he actually plays.** Accurate, and he's self-aware about it. He passes with mana open and punishes whatever the opponent commits. He's drawn to Calm's Reaction, Deflect and Shield cards and to Mind's card draw and Hidden cards. He likes having more cards in hand than his opponent more than he likes having more units on board. He's happy to let his opponent take an early Battlefield if he can take it back later with better trades. A game that goes long and ends with the opponent out of gas feels perfect to him. He hates random effects, discard at random, anything with a "maybe". He'll pass on a strong card if it adds variance. He enjoys a lot of decisions per turn and rarely takes the lead. He'd rather react. He runs few units, and the ones he plays are sturdy ones that are hard to remove.

**What would feel wrong.** Any deck whose plan is "attack every turn and hope". Chaos-style randomness or discard would bother him on principle. A deck that wins with one explosive turn would also feel off to him, because an opponent with the right answer can stop it cold.
