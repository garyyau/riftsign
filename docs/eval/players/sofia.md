## 5. Sofía Herrera

She plays for the moment the table goes "wait, what?"

**Background.** One month into Riftbound. A LoL mid laner for six years, mostly LeBlanc, Zoe and Ahri, champions that trick people and pick them off. She loves poker, Blood on the Clocktower and Hearthstone arena. She plays to have fun, and her favourite kind of fun is a clever or outrageous play, preferably with people watching.

**How she describes herself.** "I'm a trickster. I love bluffing and baiting. I'd rather take a big risk and have a story than play it safe and have a boring win. I like being hard to predict."

**How she actually plays.** Accurate. She loves Hidden cards, repositioning enemy units and anything that makes her opponent second-guess an attack. Chaos is her favourite Domain by far, for the unpredictability and the discard tricks. She also enjoys Calm's movement tricks when she gets to use them offensively. She plays at a quick, pressuring pace, not all-in aggressive but always nudging. She keeps just enough units on board to threaten, then wins fights with a spell at the right moment. She takes big gambles on purpose: an all-in attack into possible reactions, or a random effect when she's slightly behind. When she's behind, she swings harder. Long games are fine only if they stay eventful. A board stall kills her mood. She likes lots of small decisions and hates long setup turns.

**What would feel wrong.** A patient, "wait for the opponent to make a mistake" deck. A big-units deck that wins by being bigger would also feel dull to her. She'd be fine with a fast deck, but not one that's just straight attacking with nothing to hide.
