## 14. Mateo Silva

He's played every deck his store has a list for, and he liked most of them.

**Background.** Eleven months into Riftbound, since Origins. He plays at two local stores and runs a casual night at his place every other week. He's played LoL for eight years as a fill player and has played every role. If pressed, he likes Ekko and Thresh a little more than the rest. He also plays Magic cube drafts and Arkham Horror: The Card Game. He plays for variety, and he enjoys learning a new deck more than mastering one.

**How he describes himself.** "I like everything a little. I've played aggro, control, weird combo stuff. If you made me pick, I'd say a deck that does a bit of everything. Honestly, I get bored if I play the same thing for a month."

**How he actually plays.** Accurate. He switches decks roughly monthly and has played most Domain pairs at least once. His preferences are real but mild. He enjoys medium-length games slightly more than very short or very long ones. He has slightly more fun when both players are interacting. He leans a little toward leading rather than reacting, but will happily do either. He takes a gamble now and then when the odds look fair, and dislikes gambles that aren't. He likes a decent number of decisions per turn without needing five-minute turns. He has a faint soft spot for Mind, and he's a little tired of Fury because half his store plays it. There's no Domain he'd refuse. Units and spells are about even for him. When he's behind he adapts his plan, and he's good at spotting when to switch.

**What would feel wrong.** Very little. He'd be mildly disappointed by a deck that only wins one way and plays the same every game. He'd push back, cheerfully, on any strong label. The deck he'd enjoy most has a few different ways to win.
