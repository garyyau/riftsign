## 10. Grace Mwangi

She'll play almost anything, as long as it isn't that.

**Background.** Five months into Riftbound. She plays with a group of friends from her climbing gym, usually at someone's flat. She played LoL in ARAM with her brother for a couple of years and liked Lux and Ekko, but never touched ranked. She played the Pokémon TCG as a teenager, plays Magic Arena casually and has put far too many hours into Hades. She plays to have fun, and she enjoys winning with a deck she feels proud of.

**How she describes herself.** "I don't have a favourite. I've liked Fury, I've liked Mind, Calm was fine. But I won't play the one where you kill your own guys. I tried it once and felt awful the whole game."

**How she actually plays.** Her dislike is real and specific. Order's sacrifice and Deathknell cards feel bad to her, because she gets attached to her units and doesn't want to spend them. A board full of tokens feels cluttered and she loses track of what's hers. Order's effects that hit both players annoy her too. She admits Order decks beat her fairly often, which makes her like them less. Everywhere else she's loose. She's played Fury/Body, Mind/Calm and Chaos/Fury decks and liked them about equally. Chaos didn't win her over just because it's Order's opposite. She found it fun but a bit random. She likes units that feel like characters and stick around, so she protects them and trades carefully. Her games run at a medium pace with a medium number of decisions. She takes the occasional gamble when she's bored. When she's behind she tries to protect what she has rather than race. She plays slightly more units than spells.

**What would feel wrong.** Anything with Order in it, even if the rest of the deck suits her. A deck built on tokens or sacrifice would feel wrong however strong it is. Otherwise she's easy-going. Being pushed hard toward one particular style would feel like overreach, since she doesn't have one.
